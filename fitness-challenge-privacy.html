Privacy Policy for Fitness Challenge

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Fitness Challenge ("the app") is a personal fitness tracking app that helps you log daily workouts, weight, and running mileage. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it's used, and your choices.

Data we collect

If you don't create an account: the app collects no personal data at all. Everything you enter (workouts, weight, mileage) stays only on your device, in local app storage. We have no visibility into it and it is never transmitted anywhere.

If you choose to create an account (email/password, Google, or Apple sign-in), we collect:

  • Email address — used only to identify your account and let you sign in on other devices.
  • Fitness data you enter — workout completions, streaks, tier/level, weight log entries, and mileage log entries. This is the same data described above, now also stored in the cloud (Google Firebase) so it can sync across your devices and survive an app reinstall.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, device identifiers for advertising, or any data beyond what's listed above.

How we use this data

Solely to provide the app's core function: syncing your own fitness data across your own signed-in devices. We do not use your data for advertising, do not build behavioral profiles, and do not sell or share it with third parties.

Third-party services

We use Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore database) to store account and sync data. Firebase is Google's backend infrastructure; your data is subject to Google's Privacy Policy as the infrastructure provider, but is only accessed by this app on your behalf — we do not grant any other party access to it.

If you sign in with Google or Apple, those companies handle the authentication itself; we only receive your email address and a unique identifier to associate your account with your data — we never see or store your Google/Apple password.

Data security

Cloud-stored data is protected by Firebase security rules that restrict access so that only your own signed-in account can read or write your data — no other user, including us, can access it.

Your choices

  • Don't want an account? Skip sign-in entirely — the app works fully offline with local-only storage.
  • Want to delete your data? Sign in, then tap Delete My Account in the Sync Your Progress card. This permanently deletes your cloud data and your account itself — it cannot be undone. Data already saved locally on your device is unaffected and remains until you separately use Settings → Reset All Data, or uninstall the app.
  • Want to stop syncing? Sign out at any time; your data remains in the cloud for when you sign back in, but the app continues working locally without it.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
